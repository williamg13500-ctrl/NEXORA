DRAFT FOR REVIEW

Privacy notice.

WORKING DRAFT — this page requires jurisdiction-specific legal review before being treated as a final policy.

This notice describes information handled by the NEXORA GROUP website. The business controller, jurisdiction, retention periods and applicable lawful bases must be confirmed before publication as a final notice.

Information stored (for legal review)

Enquiries: name, phone/WhatsApp number, selected brand, message, optional email if supplied on the existing enquiry form, reference code, status, public update, private admin notes, created/updated timestamps, and a one-way hash of network information used for rate limiting.

Waitlist: name, phone/WhatsApp number, brand, reference code, status, admin notes, timestamp, and a one-way network-information hash used for rate limiting. TEKDiD waitlist requests are notifications only; TEKDiD remains gated.

SKYLINE booking requests: name, phone/WhatsApp number, project details, preferred timing, consent flag, reference code, status, admin notes, timestamp, and a one-way network-information hash used for rate limiting.

Anonymous click counts: brand, source (Explore or platform link), and timestamp. Click events contain no name, phone number, IP address, cookie or other visitor profile. Click events older than 90 days are pruned by a scheduled cleanup task.

Platform monitoring separately stores platform status, response time and check timestamps.

How enquiries are handled

Enquiries are reviewed in the private admin inbox. Replies are made manually by WhatsApp or phone. The website does not send email notifications and does not use an email service for enquiries.

Platform and operational data

The site may store anonymous brand click counts without visitor names, phone numbers or other personal data. Platform checks, if enabled, store platform status, response time and check timestamps. No third-party advertising trackers are intended.

Access and security

Admin functions are restricted to authorized Hatchable project administrators. Visitors can use a reference code to see only its enquiry status and any public note; private notes and contact details are not exposed on the tracking page.

Retention and deletion requests

Anonymous click events older than 90 days are deleted by scheduled pruning. Other records are retained until a retention schedule is confirmed during legal review. To request deletion of enquiry, waitlist or booking details, contact NEXORA GROUP using the published WhatsApp/phone contact and provide the relevant reference code. An authorized administrator can delete the matching record.

This working draft must be reviewed and completed by qualified counsel before publication as a final privacy notice.
