DRAFT FOR REVIEW

Privacy notice.

WORKING DRAFT — this page requires jurisdiction-specific legal review before being treated as a final policy.

This notice describes information handled by the NEXORA GROUP website. The business controller, jurisdiction, retention periods and applicable lawful bases must be confirmed before publication as a final notice.

Information submitted

When you submit an enquiry, the site stores your name, required international phone/WhatsApp number, selected brand, message, optional email address if you choose to provide one, a reference code, status, public update and admin-only private notes. A one-way hash of network information is retained to apply rate limits and help prevent spam. The enquiry is stored in the Hatchable database.

How enquiries are handled

Enquiries are reviewed in the private admin inbox. Replies are made manually by WhatsApp or phone. The website does not send email notifications and does not use an email service for enquiries.

Platform and operational data

The site may store anonymous brand click counts without visitor names, phone numbers or other personal data. Platform checks, if enabled, store platform status, response time and check timestamps. No third-party advertising trackers are intended.

Access and security

Admin functions are restricted to authorized Hatchable project administrators. Visitors can use a reference code to see only its enquiry status and any public note; private notes and contact details are not exposed on the tracking page.

Retention and your rights

FOR LEGAL REVIEW: Set retention periods, controller identity, lawful bases, jurisdiction-specific access/correction/deletion request procedures and complaint routes with qualified counsel.
