COMMON QUESTIONS

Before you get started

Which brand should I choose?

Choose SKYLINE for architecture and interiors, SCHOLARS UNIVERSITY for learning, NEXORA TECHNOLOGIES for software and IT, or TEKDiD for trading technology. If you are unsure, choose “NEXORA GROUP / Not sure yet” in the contact form.

When will I receive a response?

Response times have not been published. Share your preferred contact details and we will review your enquiry; please use the listed phone or WhatsApp contact if your request is urgent.

How are enquiries handled?

We use the information you submit to understand your request and route it to the relevant team. Please avoid including passwords, payment details or other sensitive information.

How do I start a project?

Use the contact form to describe your goal, the service you need and any important constraints. The next step is to clarify fit and scope before agreeing to work.

Does TEKDiD guarantee trading profits?

No. Trading and leveraged products involve substantial risk of loss. TEKDiD technology does not guarantee results or remove market risk. Read the full TEKDiD risk disclosure before considering trading tools.

Can I contact the group if I am not sure?

Yes. Choose “NEXORA GROUP / Not sure yet” and explain what you need; we will use that information to help route your enquiry.
